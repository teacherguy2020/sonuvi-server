Helpers: things you can say
The skill display name is Sonuvi; its Alexa invocation is sonuvi audio. Start commands with:
• Alexa, ask Sonuvi Audio ...
Playback
  • “Alexa, ask Sonuvi Audio what’s playing”
  • “Alexa, ask Sonuvi Audio to play artist John Mayer” (most reliable)
  • “Alexa, ask Sonuvi Audio to play album Continuum”
  • “Alexa, ask Sonuvi Audio to play playlist Chill Mix”
  • “Alexa, ask Sonuvi Audio to play songs by James Taylor”
  • “Alexa, ask Sonuvi Audio to play John Mayer” (you can omit artist/album/playlist specifier; Alexa will still try to match)
Local control with “locally”
When you add locally, you are controlling moOde playback on this system directly. It does not mean “play on Echo speakers.”
  • “Alexa, ask Sonuvi Audio to play John Mayer locally”
  • “Alexa, ask Sonuvi Audio to play artist Diana Krall locally”
  • “Alexa, ask Sonuvi Audio to play album Continuum locally”
  • “Alexa, ask Sonuvi Audio to play playlist Chill Mix locally”
Vibe
Requires Last.fm Vibe to be enabled/configured on the Config page.
  • “Alexa, ask Sonuvi Audio to vibe this song” (build/normalize vibe queue from current track context)
  • “Alexa, ask Sonuvi Audio to vibe this song locally” (same, but force play on moOde)
Controls
For transport, built-in commands work great:
  • “Alexa pause”
  • “Alexa resume”
  • “Alexa skip”
Skill-scoped alternatives:
  • “Alexa, ask Sonuvi Audio to shuffle”
  • “Alexa, ask Sonuvi Audio to repeat”
  • “Alexa, ask Sonuvi Audio to pause”
  • “Alexa, ask Sonuvi Audio to resume”
  • “Alexa, ask Sonuvi Audio to next”
  • “Alexa, ask Sonuvi Audio to thumbs up this track”
